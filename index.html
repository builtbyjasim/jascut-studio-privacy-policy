01About this policy

JASCUT Studio is a native Android video editor developed by Jasim Khan (Built by Jasim, “we”, “our” or “us”). This Privacy Policy explains the information the app accesses, how it is processed and stored, and the choices you have. It also explains how this policy website and support email differ from the app.

This policy describes the current Android app, package com.builtbyjasim.jascutstudio, for Android 12 and later. It does not describe unreleased features as if they already exist.

02What the app does today

The current app supports user-selected video import, native playback and preview, trimming, combining and reordering up to two clips, timeline thumbnails, seeking and zoom, and MP4 export with 720p or 1080p size settings. It can restore one saved editing session and show saved export history. Applied edits are saved; unfinished trim drafts are not a durable project backup.

Core editing and rendering run on your device without an account or JASCUT cloud processing. The current export pipeline does not add a JASCUT watermark. Export is foreground-only: leaving the app can cancel unfinished processing.

Planned, not currently available: multi-layer editing, effects, transitions, keyframes, filters, text, subtitles and photo editing. A “coming later” control does not perform that operation. Future capabilities and their data practices will be disclosed when introduced.

03Media access and local processing

You choose videos through Android’s system media picker or its document-picker fallback. JASCUT receives access to the items you select, rather than unrestricted access to your entire media library. Cancelling the picker does not import a new video.

The app makes private working copies of selected videos, including their existing audio, and reads metadata such as file name, MIME type, file size, duration, dimensions and rotation. It calculates a local content hash to identify and validate media. Local clip identifiers, trim boundaries and clip order allow the editing session to be restored.

Frames are decoded locally for previews and timeline thumbnails. Thumbnails use a bounded in-memory cache. Encoding and output verification also run locally. The app does not upload your imported videos, audio, thumbnails or edit decisions to us, and does not use them to train an AI model.

Original source videos are not edited in place. JASCUT does not promise to remove every embedded metadata field from imported or exported files; review media before sharing it.

04Permissions and device access

The current app does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts, notification or broad photo/video library permissions. It imports selected content using the access Android grants through the picker and saves its own exports through Android MediaStore.

You can decline media selection or manage applicable grants in Android settings; unavailable access may require selecting the source again.

05Storage, retention and deletion

Private app storage contains imported working copies, source metadata, the current editing session, intermediate rendered files and export-history records. Export records can include output name, creation time, settings, status, media URI, dimensions, duration, size, encoder details and processing notices. Appearance and motion preferences are stored locally in Android preferences.

Working copies can remain while referenced by the current session or single-video preview. Unreferenced working copies may be removed during session cleanup. Interrupted partial files and incomplete app-owned Gallery entries are cleaned up where possible. Completed intermediate files may remain in private storage; there is no automatic fixed retention period for every local file.

You can remove clips or replace the current session using the available editor controls. This is not a complete “erase all data” function. To remove private app data, use Android Settings → Apps → JASCUT Studio → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app. These actions can permanently erase your saved edit, working copies, preferences and local export history.

Gallery exports are separate. Completed videos saved under Movies/JASCUT Studio remain in shared media storage after app data is cleared or the app is uninstalled. Delete them separately using your Gallery or file manager. Your original videos and copies sent to other apps or services must also be managed separately.

06Android backup and other providers

Imported working copies, editing-session files, intermediate renders and export records are stored in Android’s noBackupFilesDir, which is excluded from standard Android app backup. JASCUT does not provide its own cloud backup or synchronization.

Android application backup is nevertheless enabled in the current manifest. Eligible preferences, such as appearance and motion settings, may be included in operating-system backup or device transfer, depending on your device and settings. This does not make the editing session a restorable cloud project.

A Gallery backup service may independently upload exported videos or originals. A cloud-backed system picker may download selected content through its provider. Those services act under their own settings and policies; “offline editing” does not disable them.

07Export, sharing and your content

Export creates and verifies an MP4 locally, then publishes a completed copy to Android’s shared video collection. Other apps with access under Android’s media rules may be able to read that copy.

When you choose Share or open a video in another app, JASCUT gives the chosen recipient read access to the published video through Android’s content-URI mechanism. The destination may upload, retain or further share the video under its own terms and privacy policy.

We do not receive your media simply because you export it. We do not sell your media or use it for advertising profiles. You retain your rights in your content; editing and export do not transfer ownership to us.

08Libraries, diagnostics and data collection

JASCUT uses Kotlin, Jetpack Compose and Material 3 for the native interface; AndroidX Navigation, Lifecycle and Core for app behavior; Kotlin coroutines for asynchronous work; and AndroidX Media3 ExoPlayer, Transformer and Effect libraries for playback and processing. These are embedded software libraries, not a JASCUT-hosted cloud editing service.

The current app has no integrated advertising, billing, remote analytics or crash-reporting SDK. It does not include Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Sentry, AdMob or an account backend. It does not send app-usage analytics, advertising identifiers or media to a developer-operated server.

Android and embedded libraries may produce local technical logs or diagnostics. JASCUT has no automatic log-upload service. Your operating system, device manufacturer or app store may independently process installation, security, performance or crash information under its own policies and your settings.

09Free use and future advertising

The current app is free to use and has no ads, subscriptions or in-app purchases. This describes the current release, not a promise that every future version will remain free or ad-free.

Ads may be introduced later. Before releasing advertising or another feature that materially changes data handling, we will update the relevant disclosures, identify any new SDKs and explain data use and available choices. Where required, we will request consent before the relevant processing begins. This policy does not authorize undisclosed future tracking.

10Support messages

If you email zasimkhan3@gmail.com, we receive the email address, message and attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond, investigate the issue and manage related correspondence. Please avoid sending sensitive media or unnecessary personal information.

Support correspondence is handled through email providers, including Gmail, under their own privacy practices. It may be processed outside your country. We retain correspondence only as needed to address the request, maintain relevant support records or meet applicable legal obligations. You can ask us to delete correspondence, subject to those obligations. There is no app account or server-side project library for us to delete.

11This website and external services

This policy website is separate from the Android app. It contains no analytics scripts, advertising, forms, external fonts, embedded media or site-authored cookies. Its styles and branding assets are served with the pages.

When hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub receives technical request information, including visitors’ IP addresses, to deliver and secure the site. GitHub handles that information under its Privacy Statement. Visiting a policy page therefore involves an Internet request even though editing in JASCUT works offline.

Links you choose to open, email providers, app stores and sharing destinations operate under their own policies. See also Google’s Privacy Policy for Google-operated services you independently use.

12Your choices and privacy rights

You control which videos to import, which edits to apply, whether to export or share, and whether to clear private app storage or uninstall. Keep independent copies of media you want to preserve. Revoking access does not automatically erase existing working copies; clearing private app storage removes those copies.

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of personal information we hold, to object to or restrict certain processing, to withdraw consent where processing relies on it, and to complain to your local data-protection authority. Contact us to exercise applicable rights relating to support correspondence or other information we actually hold.

We cannot remotely inspect, retrieve or delete media stored only on your device, or remove copies held by a recipient you selected. We may request information reasonably necessary to verify a request. Statutory rights are not limited by this policy.

13Children and family use

JASCUT is a general-purpose creative tool and is not specifically directed to children. It does not provide accounts or a developer-operated service that knowingly collects children’s media. Local editing can still involve personal information about people appearing in a video.

Where local law requires parental permission or supervision, a parent or guardian should oversee use and sharing. If you believe a child has sent personal information to our support address, contact us so we can review and address it.

14Security and legal disclosures

Private working files use Android’s application storage protections. This is not a promise of separate app-level encryption or absolute security. Shared exports are outside that private area. Protect your device, keep it updated and use care when sending media to other people or services.

No software, device or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. We may disclose information actually held by us when required by applicable law or to address lawful requests and protect legal rights. This does not give us remote access to your local projects.

We will revise the date on this page and provide additional notice or consent where required for material changes.
