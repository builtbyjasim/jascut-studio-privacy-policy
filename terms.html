02Current features and limitations

JASCUT is a native Android editor for Android 12 and later, package com.builtbyjasim.jascutstudio. The current app imports selected videos, previews them, trims, combines and reorders up to two clips, provides timeline thumbnails, seeking and zoom, and exports MP4 videos to Gallery with sharing options.

The app restores one saved editing session and maintains local export history. It does not currently provide a library of multiple named projects. Apply a trim to save that edit; an unapplied draft is not a durable backup. Preview mute controls playback only and does not remove audio from the exported video.

Export requires the app to remain in the foreground; backgrounding can cancel an unfinished job. Output depends on the source, chosen settings and available device encoders. 720p/1080p settings are size limits, not a guarantee that smaller sources are upscaled. Codec support, seeking accuracy, duration, frame rate, picture and audio may vary. Check each export before relying on it.

Multi-layer editing, effects, transitions, keyframes, filters, text, subtitles and future photo editing are planned. They are not currently available or a commitment to a release date. The current editing experience adds no JASCUT watermark.
